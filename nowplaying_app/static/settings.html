JPlay Players

Your JPlay players (address and port of the player's web page, usually 8080). The display shows one of them, full screen: pick it with the round button (or later from the menu on the display). While it's idle, the display shows what the microphone hears. Untick a player to leave it out.

While a player plays the radio, this unit listens to the stream (10 seconds every half minute, and when the station names a new song) and recognises the song. That beats what many stations send: only their own name, or the show mixed into the artist. Needs ffmpeg.

Lyrics

From LRCLIB (lrclib.net). When the lyrics have times, the line being sung is highlighted and the text follows the music; otherwise it scrolls slowly through the song. Songs without lyrics look as before.

If the highlighted line runs ahead of the singing, lower this (e.g. -1.5); if it lags, raise it. Mostly needed for radio and records, where the place in the song comes from recognising it.

JPlay Spins

Reports what this room hears and plays to JPlay Records, which keeps one history for all rooms (records you put on, radio and other streams). Create a listener for this unit in JPlay Records → Settings → Listeners and paste its token here.

While this player plays, JPlay Spins files the songs under its source: Radio, Plex, Spotify or Bluetooth, and a CD in the player as your CD. When it's idle, what the microphone hears is matched against your records and CDs as before.
